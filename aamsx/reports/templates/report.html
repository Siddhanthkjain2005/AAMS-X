Limitations

  • Occupancy labels are thresholded measurements, not verified ground truth. A cell near its decision threshold may be mislabelled either way.
  • Regions are abstract equal-channel-count slices of whichever band the current receiver covers, so region i means different frequencies in different segments. That is what makes cross-station splicing meaningful, and it is also why no frequency-specific claim should be read out of these results.
  • Results are conditional on the reward weights printed above. The ranking of policies changes with them; the Pareto section exists so the trade-off is visible instead of hidden inside one scalar.
  • AAMS-X measures scheduling on replayed public science data. It makes no claim about operational interception performance.
